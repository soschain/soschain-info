Three situations where SOSChain helps

Information and contacts available when they are needed.

The person is unresponsive

A police officer or healthcare professional assists a person who is unresponsive or disoriented.

Using the SOSChain identifier, the professional can start a private call to the person's emergency contacts. Authorised clinical professionals can request their emergency information.

Only the animal's QR code is available

The person does not have their card, but the animal with them is wearing its SOSChain Quick Response (QR) code.

Using the animal's SOSChain QR code, emergency services can start a private call to authorised contacts without revealing phone numbers.

The family is waiting for news

A person has entered a medical service, and their family does not know where they are or what is happening.

Authorised people can follow their journey through the emergency department, tests and ward as the service updates it, and receive messages and calls.

Private calls: SOSChain connects the call without displaying the personal phone number of either the caller or the contact.
